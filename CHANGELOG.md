# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

The first release.

- Projects: a library of events on desktop (files in the app's data
  folder) or in the browser, with automatic backups and restoring of
  earlier versions and deleted projects.
- Import: a spreadsheet template with English or Finnish headers; other
  columns are mapped by hand once and remembered; export back to the
  same template.
- Wishes: suggestions found in sign-up text, avecs, group and table
  wishes, the planner's own "keep together" and "keep apart" preferences,
  placement rules, pins and "avoid table ends".
- Seating: a tabu search on every CPU core (one in the browser), live
  progress, Stop, "Keep searching" and alternative options; seating
  policy for mixing organizations, lone guests and spare seats.
- Editing: drag to move or swap with a score preview, pins, group
  moves, late changes (walk-ins, cancellations, replacements), undo.
- Views: seating chart, connections graph, per-person wish status.
- Prints: seating chart, kitchen, bar and door lists as A4 PDFs.
- Interface in English and Finnish, light and dark themes, a first-run
  tour and per-page help.
